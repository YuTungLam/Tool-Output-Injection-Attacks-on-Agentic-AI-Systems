Why original-table reproduction is a different target

Our declared setting uses AgentDojo and Groq gpt-oss. Original scenario membership, labels, exact execution and audit recipes, framework pins and baseline adaptations are not fully verified. The public-artifact audit distinguishes an unlocated release from proof that no release exists.

Independent human labeling is not a prerequisite for implementing the method. Natural-trajectory attribution accuracy still requires appropriate reference evidence.

What would make a research-gap claim credible?
  1. Finish and verify the relevant implementation.
  2. Preserve a reproducible failing case with a clear reference contract.
  3. Check plausible specification choices and setting differences.
  4. Test fresh cases and relevant alternatives before claiming a general limitation or novelty.
