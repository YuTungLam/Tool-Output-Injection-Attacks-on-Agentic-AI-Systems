Measurement scope

Saved HTTP responses are replayed with strict checks on every outbound JSON request. Tools execute in reinitialized simulated environments. Timing includes the native benchmark, observer work during execution, and the final-state snapshot shared by both conditions. It excludes networking, quota waits, initialization, recorder open/close, audit, and HTML export.

Calendar operations generate notification emails. Their system timestamp is fixed to 2024-01-01 in both offline conditions; live experiments are unchanged. Earlier replays without clock control produced different hashes and are retained as diagnostics, excluded from this table.
